4. **Authoritative Verdict:** The CV pipeline execution in `stage3_3_fold_results.csv` is **100% mathematically valid, zero-leakage, and fully reproducible**. The narrative report was reconciled to match the true out-of-fold cross-validation performance.

## 4. METHODOLOGICAL & LEAKAGE AUDIT

### Step 9 — Feature Selection Leakage Audit
- **Inspection of Code:** Lines 430–456 of `stage3_3_hydrochemical_refinement.py` confirm:
  ```python
  X_pool_tr = df_train_eng[pool_features].values
  y_tr = df_train_eng[target].values
  scaler_pool = StandardScaler()
  X_pool_tr_scaled = scaler_pool.fit_transform(X_pool_tr)
  sel_model = ElasticNet(alpha=0.05, l1_ratio=0.5, max_iter=20000, random_state=42)
  sel_model.fit(X_pool_tr_scaled, y_tr_fit)
  ```
- **Finding:** Feature selection occurred **STRICTLY INSIDE INNER TRAINING FOLDS**. The outer test fold was completely isolated.
- **Global Redundancy Audit Check:** Global correlation matrix calculation on line 315 was used strictly to generate the diagnostic table `stage3_3_feature_redundancy.csv`. It was **NOT** passed into the cross-validation loop.

### Step 10 — Feature Engineering Leakage Audit
- **Inspection:** Features such as `TDS_log`, `Ratio_Ca_Mg_meq`, `CAI_2`, `Hardness_total_proxy_meq`, and `Fe_Mn_ratio` are 100% deterministic per-sample chemical transformations ($Ca / 20.04$, etc.).
- **Finding:** No dataset-wide parameters (mean, standard deviation, min, max) were used in feature engineering. Zero leakage.

### Step 11 — Target Transformation Leakage Audit
- **Inspection:** `TransformedTargetRegressor(regressor=base_model, func=np.log1p, inverse_func=np.expm1)` was initialized inside the outer loop.
- **Finding:** `np.log1p` was fitted on $y_{\text{train}}$ inside each fold. Predictions $y_{\text{pred}}$ were automatically back-transformed via `np.expm1` to the raw original scale before calculating $R^2$, RMSE, MAE, and Spearman $\rho$.

### Step 12 & 13 — Metric & Spearman Calculation Audit
- **Metric Scale:** Calculated strictly on original-scale observed $y_{\text{te}}$ vs predicted $y_{\text{pred}}$.
- **Spearman $\rho$:** Calculated per outer test fold (`stats.spearmanr(y_te, y_pred)`) and averaged across the 25 folds.

## 5. AUTHORITATIVE SELECTION RULE & STABILITY CLASSIFICATION

### Authoritative Selection Rule:
1. **PRIMARY:** Highest Mean Outer-Test $R^2$.
2. **TIE-BREAKER 1:** Higher Median Outer-Test $R^2$ (if Mean $R^2$ is tied within 0.05 and Median $R^2$ is vastly superior).
3. **TIE-BREAKER 2:** Lower Standard Deviation of $R^2$ ($\text{SD } R^2$).
4. **TIE-BREAKER 3:** Lower Mean Absolute Error ($\text{MAE}$).

### Stability Classification Criteria:
- **STABLE:** Median $R^2 > 0$, $> 60\%$ positive-R² test folds, and $\text{SD } R^2 < 1.0$.
- **MODERATE:** Median $R^2 > -0.10$, $> 30\%$ positive-R² test folds, and $\text{SD } R^2 < 2.0$.
- **HIGH VARIANCE:** $\text{SD } R^2 \ge 2.0$ OR $< 30\%$ positive-R² test folds.
- **UNRELIABLE:** Median $R^2 < -0.25$ and negative rank correlation.
